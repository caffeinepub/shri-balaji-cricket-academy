# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Build a single-page marketing website for Shri Balaji Cricket Academy with a cricket-themed design, showcasing programs, gallery, and an enquiry form.

**Planned changes:**
- Sticky navigation bar with smooth-scrolling links (Home, About, Programs, Gallery, Contact) and academy name/logo, responsive on mobile
- Hero section with academy name "Shri Balaji Cricket Academy", tagline, and "Enroll Now" CTA button linking to the contact form
- About Us section describing the academy's mission and coaching philosophy
- Coaching Programs section highlighting available programs with minimum age requirement of 7 years clearly stated
- Gallery section with a responsive image grid showcasing academy activities
- Contact/Enquiry form with fields for child's name, parent's name, phone number, child's age (validated ≥ 7), and message; stores submissions in backend and shows success confirmation; academy contact details displayed alongside
- Deep green and gold cricket-themed design with strong display typography applied consistently across all sections

**User-visible outcome:** Visitors can learn about the academy, browse programs and gallery, and parents can submit an enrollment enquiry directly from the website.
